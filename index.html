How I got here

Born in Florida, raised mostly in Virginia and Michigan, I earned a B.S. in Computer Science and Engineering from The Ohio State University, where NSBE, Lambda Psi, and the Morrill Scholars Program taught me that engineering is a team sport.

I joined Google on Modern Creators and Formats (Discover ‘Notes’ to 1M+ users), moved to Search Intelligence (creator verification and a 30+ component library), then YouTube Playables — building the client foundation for a generative AI game creation product from an empty repository. The pattern: arrive early, structure the client, and make it safe for many engineers to ship into it at once.
